With Beijing Tianyun Rehabilitation Center for Children with Disabilities

艾草锤Mugwort Hammer

天云的孩子们有着不同的障碍，有的有听力障碍，有的是唐氏综合征。志愿者陪着他们把艾草塞进布里、扎紧、装上木柄，一把艾草锤往往要做很久。

The children at Tianyun live with a range of disabilities, including hearing loss and Down syndrome. With volunteers beside them, they pack mugwort into cloth, tie it tight and fix it to a wooden handle. A single hammer can take a long time.

艾草、棉布、木柄 · 制作人：天云的孩子们

Mugwort, cotton, wood · Made by the children of Tianyun

探访岩羊

岩羊 Blue Sheep 小店里的九个角落Nine corners of the Blue Sheep shop

点击了解岩羊的故事 Read the Blue Sheep story 收起 Close

岩羊 Blue Sheep 是一家遵循公平贸易的可持续型社会企业。它把残障等弱势群体的手工艺人，和本地与全球的市场连在一起，用这种方式守护中国的文化遗产。我们在做应手的时候，常常想起这家小店。

Blue Sheep is a fair-trade, sustainable social enterprise. It connects artisans from vulnerable groups, including people with disabilities, with local and global markets, and in doing so helps protect China's cultural heritage. We often think of this little shop while building Enso Initiative.

创始人 RachelThe founder

岩羊的创始人 Rachel 是一位英国奶奶，曾是行走在喜马拉雅山脉的“无国界医生”。她年轻时就致力于帮助那些得不到医疗服务的人，在 15 个国家做过医疗工作，也培训当地的乡村医生。这些经历让她看到极度贫困、没有选择的人是怎样生活的，也让她明白：想改善他们的健康，一个重要的办法是减轻他们的经济压力。

Rachel, a grandmother from England, was once a “doctor without borders” walking the Himalayas. From a young age she worked to help people with no access to healthcare, practicing in 15 countries and training village doctors along the way. It showed her how people live in extreme poverty with no choices, and taught her that one of the most important ways to improve their health is to ease their financial strain.

后来她参与了汶川大地震的救灾，并用自己的积蓄创办了 Blue Sheep，帮助灾区和其他需要帮助的人。走进岩羊那扇开着花的木门，第一眼就能看到 Rachel 的照片，旁边写着：“用小爱做大事”。2025 年 4 月，Rachel 因病回到英国老家，现在小店由其他店员接着运营。

She later took part in the relief work after the Wenchuan earthquake and founded Blue Sheep with her own savings, to help the disaster area and others in need. Past the flowering wooden gate, the first thing you see is a photo of Rachel, with the words “Do big things with small love.” In April 2025 Rachel returned to her home in England because of illness, and the shop is now run by other staff.

使命Their mission

小店的小册子会发给每一位进门的顾客，上面写着他们的使命：

  • 促进创新：购买残障人士和其他弱势群体的手工产品，再以公平的价格卖出，改善他们的健康和生计。

    Encourage innovation: buy products handmade by people with disabilities and other vulnerable groups, and sell them at a fair price to improve their health and livelihoods.

  • 确保每一位生产者，不论能力如何，都有机会做出健康的选择，发挥最大的潜能。

    Make sure every producer, whatever their ability, can make healthy choices and reach their full potential.

  • 力求以平等、包容、尊重和友爱，服务每一个人。

    Serve everyone with equality, inclusion, respect and kindness.

我看到的What I saw

他们是真的做到了。每一件作品都有做它的人，每位手艺人都有一张名片，上面写着自己的介绍和故事。结账的叔叔面对一大片名片，一下子就能找出这件东西的制作者。

They truly live up to it. Every piece has a maker, and every maker has a card with their introduction and story. Faced with a whole wall of cards, the man at the till found the maker of my piece at once.

门口坐轮椅的叔叔，从我进来起就一直一丝不苟地拿着锤子和钉子，一点一点敲着陶板，做精美的垫片。那匹马图案的，就是他做的。

By the door, a man in a wheelchair worked with a hammer and nails, tapping a clay plate bit by bit, from the moment I walked in until I left. He makes the lovely coasters, and the one with the horse is his.

店里有几张座位，整体很温馨。桌上放着几本留言册，写满了来自四面八方的人留下的话，每一页都满满的，像是各地的人给小店的爱。门外的书架上是大家捐出的二手物品，多是二手书，自由捐赠，按需取用。

There are a few seats inside, and the whole place feels warm. On the tables lie several guest notebooks, their pages crammed with words from people all over, like love sent in from everywhere. On the shelf outside are secondhand things donated by visitors, mostly books: give freely, take what you need.

这是一个充满爱心的地方，有时间的话，欢迎多去逛逛。

It is a place full of love. If you have time, do go and wander around.

— Julia

北京天云助残疾人康复中心里，大家一起做手工皂Making handmade soap together at Beijing Tianyun, a rehabilitation center for people with disabilities

点击了解天云 Read about Tianyun 收起 Close

北京天云助残疾人康复中心在北京市房山区。照片里，大家正在一起做手工皂：把皂基融化，调好颜色，倒进各种形状的模具，等它凝固，再一块一块包好。

Beijing Tianyun, a rehabilitation center for people with disabilities, is in Fangshan District, Beijing. In these photos everyone is making handmade soap together: melting the base, mixing in color, pouring it into molds of all shapes, waiting for it to set, and wrapping each piece by hand.

About

关于我们

应手 Enso Initiative 是一个学生发起的非营利公益项目，帮残障手艺人把亲手做的作品，连同他们的名字和故事，带给更多人。

Enso Initiative is a student-led nonprofit organization that brings handmade work by makers with disabilities, including children, to the world, along with their names and stories.

使命Mission

让残障手艺人的手艺被尊重、被署名，并带来真实的收入。

To see the craft of makers with disabilities respected, credited, and turned into real income.

名字Our name

Ensō（円相）是一个日语词，指禅宗书画里一笔画成的圆，意思是心手合一。中文名「应手」出自《庄子·天道》：「得之于手而应于心。」两个名字，说的是同一件事。

Ensō is a Japanese word for the circle drawn in a single brushstroke in Zen calligraphy. It means the heart and the hand moving as one. Our Chinese name, 应手, comes from the Zhuangzi: "The hand gets it, and the heart answers." Both names say the same thing.

朱雅暄Julia Zhu

Founder & CEO · 创始人兼 CEO

北京师范大学附属实验中学国际部 2029 届。我六岁开始在北京天云残障儿童康复中心做志愿者，后来和那里的孩子一起做艾草锤。我相信，最好的帮助是让人有能力帮助自己。我希望陪伴残障朋友学会一门手艺，靠自己的劳动获得报酬、养活自己。社会的关爱依然珍贵，我希望它成为他们走向自立的起点。

Class of 2029, International Division, Experimental High School Attached to Beijing Normal University. I have volunteered at Tianyun since I was six, and began making mugwort hammers with the children there. I believe the best help is helping people help themselves. I want to support makers with disabilities as they learn a craft, earn a fair wage from their own work, and support themselves. Care from society still matters, and I hope it can be where their independence begins.
